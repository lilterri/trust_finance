Privacy Policy

At TrustBank, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our banking services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal identification information (Name, address, email, phone number, date of birth, Social Security Number, etc.)
  • Account information (account numbers, balances, transaction history)
  • Financial information (income, credit history, employment details)
  • Online activity (IP address, browser type, device information, cookies, usage data)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our banking services
  • To process transactions and manage your accounts
  • To communicate with you about your account, products, and services
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • To improve our website, products, and services
  • For fraud prevention, security, and risk management

3. How We Share Your Information

  • With service providers and partners who assist in operating our business
  • With regulatory authorities and law enforcement as required by law
  • With affiliates and subsidiaries for business purposes
  • With third parties for marketing (only with your consent)

4. Your Privacy Rights

  • You may access, update, or correct your personal information
  • You may opt out of marketing communications
  • You may request deletion of your account (subject to legal requirements)

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption, firewalls, and secure servers, to protect your information. Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage, and deliver personalized content. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at privacy@trustbank.com.
