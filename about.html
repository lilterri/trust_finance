About TrustBank

For nearly four decades, we've been helping individuals, families, and businesses achieve their financial goals through personalized service and innovative banking solutions.

Our Story

Founded in 1985 with a single branch and a simple mission—to provide honest, reliable banking services—TrustBank has grown into one of the nation's most respected financial institutions.

Today, we serve over 2 million customers across 500+ branches, managing more than $50 billion in assets while maintaining our personal customer-first approach.
